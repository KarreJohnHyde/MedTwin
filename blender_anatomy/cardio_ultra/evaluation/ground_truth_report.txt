Documentation Tips
 Ensure all data is recorded in a systematic manner (SOAP notes: Subjective,
Objective, Assessment, Plan).
 Use open-ended questions initially to allow the patient to elaborate, and then follow
up with specific, focused questions.
 Observe non-verbal cues such as facial expressions, breathing pattern, and posture.
 This pattern ensures comprehensive history taking, which is essential for accurate
diagnosis and effective care planning in a cardiology ward.
